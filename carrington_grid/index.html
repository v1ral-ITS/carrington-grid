Reflection

The CSS Grid concept that took me the longest to understand was how auto-fit works together with minmax. At first, I was confused. After researching MDN documentation, I learned that auto-fit creates as many columns as will fit while minmax prevents them from becoming too small. One thing I had to look up was the difference between object-fit: cover and object-fit: contain because I wanted the photos to remain visually appealing without distortion, and honestly I am addicted to programming of all kinds. It is useful because featured content can be made larger while the layout automatically adjusts for different screen sizes without requiring separate mobile-specific code. Which I just so happen to recently battle with my https://codepolisher.app website. The first time I looked at it on omy phone, I panicked.
